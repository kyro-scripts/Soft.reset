Privacy Policy

What we collect when you message us or request a session, how we use it, and what we never ask for.

Last updated November 15, 2026.

What we collect

We only ask for what we need to reply to you and run your session:

  • Your first name, or the name you go by
  • Where you'd like us to reply
  • Your username there, if you choose to add one
  • What's going on with your PC, and a preferred time if you pick one

How the website handles your information

  • This website doesn't store your messages. When you message us on TikTok, Instagram, or Discord, your message goes to that platform and reaches us there. Those platforms' privacy rules also apply.
  • When you send the request form and it confirms that your request was sent, its details go to a private Discord server where we read requests. Discord's privacy rules also apply to that server. If the form doesn't confirm, nothing has been sent, and it gives you a message to send yourself instead.

How we use it

We use your information to reply to you, agree on a service, price, and time, and run your session. We don't sell your information or use it for advertising.

How long we keep request details:

During a session

During a session we can see what's on your screen. We only look at the files the service you chose needs, and only with your permission. We don't delete, rename, or move important files without your approval.

What we never ask for

  • Passwords
  • Payment card details
  • Codes sent to your phone or email
  • Sensitive files

The one-time session code is the only code we ask for, and only during a session you've booked and are attending.

Analytics and trackers

This website doesn't use advertising trackers, and it doesn't run analytics. If we turn analytics on, this page will say so and describe what it measures.

Third parties

  • Fonts: the site loads its fonts from Google Fonts. Google receives your IP address when the page loads.
  • Platforms: TikTok, Instagram, and Discord handle messages you send through them under their own rules.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, or ask us to delete your request details. Message us on any channel listed on our contact page, and tell us which request it's about.

Changes to this policy

We'll update this page if we change how the website or our requests work. The date at the top shows the latest version.
