How it works

How a remote session works

You stay in control at every step. Nothing is changed without your approval.

  1. 1

    Explain your issue

    Tell us what's happening with your PC, in your own words. Message us, or send the request form.

  2. 2

    Choose a service

    Pick the service that fits the problem. The price and what's included are shown before you decide.

  3. 3

    Review the price and session details

    We agree on the service, the price, and a time with you by message before anything starts.

  4. 4

    Approve the remote connection

    We don't connect unless you are there and have agreed to it. You can end the connection at any time.

  5. 5

    Review proposed changes

    Before we change anything, we tell you what we plan to do and why. Each change needs your approval.

  6. 6

    Receive a summary

    We explain the work we completed, what we changed, and anything we recommend you check later.

Your part at each stage

Before, during, and after

Before the session

  • You describe the issue.
  • The service and the price are agreed on.
  • We explain how the connection works before it starts.

During the session

  • You stay present.
  • Changes need your approval.
  • Your personal information is respected.
  • You can end the connection at any time.

After the session

  • We explain the work we completed.
  • We record what we did accurately. Not every issue can be fixed, and we say so when that's the case.
  • You're welcome to give feedback.
Privacy and access

What we can and can't access

What we can see during a session

  • Your screen, while you're connected and present.
  • The settings and apps that the service you chose covers.
  • Files only where the service needs them, and only with your permission.

What we don't do

  • Ask for your passwords or sign-in codes.
  • Access personal accounts, such as email, banking, or social media.
  • Open, move, or delete files outside what the service needs, without your approval.
  • Connect without you there and agreeing to it.
  • Install software you haven't agreed to.

Staying safe

Remote access always carries some risk. Only connect with us through the channels on our contact page, and end the session if anything seems wrong.

We will never ask for your password, payment card details, or a remote-access code by message. If someone asks for any of these and says they're from softreset, don't share them.
